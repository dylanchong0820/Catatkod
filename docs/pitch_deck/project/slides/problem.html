The problem

Malaysia is digitising every clinic. The typing lands on nurses.

RM1 billion

Persada digital health initiative for 150 hospitals and 2,488 facilities to 2028, raised from RM650 million in Aug 2026

2,917 clinics

planned on the cloud clinic system (CCMS) by 2028: every visit becomes a digital record

“Overtime to keep records accurate”

Staff report manual work and slow systems (Malay Mail)

Sources: NST & The Sun, Jun 2026 (Persada); The Star, Aug 2026 (RM1 bil); Malay Mail, Jul 2026 (CCMS 2,917 by 2028); Malay Mail, May 2024 (TPC-OHCIS workload)

Malaysia is spending one billion ringgit to put every public clinic and hospital on a digital record by 2028. That’s good news, but someone has to type every visit into those systems, and that’s the nurses and doctors who are already stretched. Staff in clinics that are already digitised report doing manual work and staying back to keep records accurate.
