The money for clinic digitalisation is already allocated. CatatKod is a module that makes that investment work for the people typing the records: licensed to KKM and state health departments, or offered as an add-on through CCMS implementation partners. Next, we validate with real clinicians and de-identified notes, then grow the number of trained codes and add an ICD-10 crosswalk for systems still on ICD-10.
